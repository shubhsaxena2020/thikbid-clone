Consent wording v1 · Effective 18 October 2026

How you opt in

Saying “Hi” to ThikBid does not sign you up. After you share your work and districts, we send you the message below. Alerts start only after you reply YES. We note the date, time and wording version of your reply.

The exact wording you agree to

“I agree to receive ThikBid tender alerts, reminders and related service messages on WhatsApp at this number, based on the preferences I share. I can reply STOP at any time to stop, PAUSE to pause, or CHANGE to update my preferences. I have read the Privacy notice and Terms at thikbid.in. Reply YES to agree.”

Assamese wording will be added when alerts in Assamese begin.

What you will receive

  • One daily digest (usually around 7 AM) with tenders that match your preferences, each linking to the official notice. No digest is sent on days with no matches.
  • Reminders before the last date for tenders you ask us to track.
  • Corrigendum updates for tenders you are tracking.
  • Replies to your questions, and payment messages if you choose a paid plan.

We don’t send ads for other businesses, and we don’t add you to WhatsApp groups.

Your controls

STOP
Stops all alerts. We confirm once, then send nothing more.
PAUSE
Pauses alerts until you send START.
CHANGE
Update your work, districts or language.
DELETE
Removes your profile within 30 days. See the Privacy notice.

During the pilot, these replies are handled by hand, usually within a few hours and always before the next digest.
