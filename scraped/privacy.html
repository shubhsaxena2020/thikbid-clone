Who we are

ThikBid is a WhatsApp tender-alert service for small contractors and suppliers. It is run by Pritam Sarma, an individual developer based in Assam, India (“ThikBid”, “we”, “us”). For this notice, we are the party that decides how your information is used.

What we collect

  • Your WhatsApp number and the name on your WhatsApp profile, when you message us.
  • The preferences you tell us: your type of work, districts, rough tender size you can take on, registration class or experience band if you share it, and your language.
  • Your consent and message choices: when you opted in, the wording you agreed to, and any STOP, PAUSE or CHANGE request.
  • Our chat with you: messages you send us, such as questions about a tender.
  • Payment details if you pay: amount, date, plan and payment reference. Card, UPI PIN and bank details are handled by the payment provider. We never see or store them.
  • How you found us: a short tag such as [poster-ghy] in your first message, so we know which poster or link worked.

We do not ask for Aadhaar, PAN, bank statements or bid documents to send you alerts.

This website

The website does not use advertising or analytics cookies, and it does not have a sign-up form. Your browser remembers two small things on your own device: your language choice, and the source tag from the link you used (for 30 days). Neither is sent to us unless you send it in the WhatsApp message. The site is served by a hosting provider that may keep normal server logs (such as IP address and pages visited) for security.

Why we use it

  • To pick tenders that match your work and area, and send you the daily digest and reminders you asked for.
  • To answer your questions and check whether a tender may suit you.
  • To take payment and keep records the law requires.
  • To honour your STOP, PAUSE and delete requests.
  • To improve the service, for example by learning which alerts were useful.

We rely on your consent, given when you opt in on WhatsApp. You can withdraw it at any time. Withdrawing does not affect messages already sent.

During the pilot

In the pilot, ThikBid is run by hand. We read official tender portals, prepare your digest and send it from the WhatsApp Business app. Your details are kept in a private spreadsheet that only the operator can open, with two-step login turned on. Later versions will move to a secured database. We will update this notice before that happens.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your information or share it with advertisers or other contractors. We use these service providers, and only for running ThikBid:

  • Meta (WhatsApp): delivers messages between you and us.
  • Google: stores the pilot spreadsheet.
  • Payment provider (such as Razorpay or UPI): processes payments if you choose a paid plan.
  • Website host: serves this website.

Some of these providers may store data outside India. We may also share information if Indian law or a lawful government order requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your profile while you are subscribed. If you send STOP, we keep only your number and the STOP record, so we don’t message you again by mistake. If you ask us to delete your data, we remove your profile and chat notes within 30 days. Payment records are kept for as long as Indian tax law requires.

Your rights

Under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can:

  • ask what information we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct or update it (reply CHANGE on WhatsApp);
  • withdraw consent (reply STOP) or ask us to delete your data;
  • name someone to act for you if you are unable to;
  • complain to us, and if you are not satisfied, to the Data Protection Board of India.
